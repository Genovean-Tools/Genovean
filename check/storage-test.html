How to run the test

1Open this page inside a Systeme.io lesson on your phone. Type a few words in the box. Press Refresh this page.
2Read the green or red result panel. Green means the text survived. Red means it did not.
3Now open the same page at its own address, not inside the lesson. Note whether your words from step 1 appear, and note what the panel says.
4Type something different there, refresh, and check the result again.
5Close the browser completely, come back tomorrow, and open both again. This is the part that matters most, because it is the only way to see whether the browser quietly clears the data after a day.
6Send Claude the result panel wording from each one, plus whether your text was still there.
